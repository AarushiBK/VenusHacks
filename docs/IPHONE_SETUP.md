# iPhone setup for best rPPG scans

## Settings to change

### Battery & performance
- **Turn off Low Power Mode** while scanning (Settings → Battery).
- **Plug in / charge** — you already did this; it helps keep frame rate stable.

### Camera access
- **Safari only** for the demo (Chrome on iOS is slower).
- Settings → Safari → Camera → **Ask** or **Allow**.
- On the scan page: tap **aA** in the address bar → Website Settings → **Camera → Allow**.

### Screen
- **Brightness** — turn up; auto-brightness can dim the feed.
- **True Tone / Night Shift** — optional off during demo for consistent color.

### Do not
- Lock screen mid-scan.
- Switch apps during the 30s scan.
- Cover the front camera.

---

## Lighting (most important)

| Best | OK | Avoid |
|------|-----|--------|
| Face a window (daylight on face) | Lamp in front of you | Window behind you (backlit) |
| Even light on **forehead** | Ceiling light + lamp | Dark room |
| Indirect soft daylight | Bathroom mirror lights | Flickering LED only |

**Tip:** The pulse signal is tiny. The camera needs steady, even light on your forehead and cheeks.

---

## How to hold the phone

- Prop phone at eye level (both hands or stand).
- Forehead and cheeks inside the green circle.
- Hold **still** for the full 30 seconds.
- Normal breathing — do not hold your breath.

---

## Validate results

After scan: count wrist pulse 15 seconds, multiply by 4, compare to app BPM (±8 BPM is reasonable for a hackathon demo).
